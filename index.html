How I work

01

Start from the problem

Understand the people and the workflow first, and agree on what a useful result looks like before choosing a model.

02

Measure, don't assume

Judge a system on real questions, retrieval quality, answer accuracy and latency, not on how good the demo looks.

03

Make it reliable

Ground answers in sources, plan for failure cases and add automated tests so the system behaves predictably.

04

Keep it practical

Choose the simplest design that solves the problem and that a team can run and maintain.

Flagship Project

ANNAM.AI, IIT Ropar · Research internship · Hackathon

AgriWaste2Fuel

An AI platform that turns a photo or description of farm waste into a treatment recommendation (biogas, compost or biofuel), the CO₂e it avoids, its carbon-credit value and a downloadable sustainability certificate.

Problem
Farm waste is often burned or dumped. Farmers need a simple way to choose a better treatment and see its environmental and economic benefit.
My role
AI Research Intern. Built the YOLOv8 multimodal classifier, the recommendation engine and the carbon-emission regression models; contributed to the FastAPI and React application.
How it works
Image (YOLOv8) and text classification → recommendation with priority scoring and rule-based optimisation → CO₂e and carbon-credit estimates (models evaluated with MAE and RMSE) → PDF certificate.
Results
Classifier reported 92.4% accuracy and an F1-score of 0.91. 3rd place at the ANNAM.AI Hackathon 2025 and a funded research internship (see Awards).

The repository includes the FastAPI backend with its endpoint and integration test scripts, the React frontend, the ML models, setup instructions and an MIT licence.

Trinetra AI

A travel assistant that plans day-by-day itineraries with an LLM and checks them against retrieved tourism knowledge.

Problem
Travel plans break when weather, crowds or routes change, and generic chatbots answer confidently but wrongly.
My role
Project lead for Team Trinetra AI.
Approach
LLM itinerary planning with backup plans, a RAG layer over verified tourism knowledge to reduce hallucinations, and a community layer for local ground-truth updates, built as separate modules.
Outcome

Personal project · Sole author

SmartMess AI

Predicts how many residents will actually eat, not just who said they would, to cut food waste in PG kitchens.

Problem
PG kitchens cook from attendance estimates that are often wrong, so food is wasted every day.
My role
Sole author: design, modelling and implementation.
Approach
Behavioural modelling with per-resident reliability scores, walk-in prediction, a feedback-learning loop and cold-start handling; multi-tenant design with unique PG codes and a 7-table database.
Outcome
Working Streamlit prototype, with source and README on GitHub.
